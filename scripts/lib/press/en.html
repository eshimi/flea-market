The idea

Why Japan’s local markets?

Japan is more than its best-known attractions. A neighborhood market can reveal the rhythm of a town: a favorite snack, an old tool, a handmade object, or a conversation with a local seller. We make these experiences easier to find and plan for.

01 / DISCOVER

Go beyond the usual stops

Find flea markets, antique fairs, handmade markets and local bazaars across Japan, including events that rarely appear in international travel guides.

02 / PLAN

Know before you go

Check event dates, times, venues, admission details and application information when those facts are available from official sources.

03 / RESPECT

Explore thoughtfully

Markets are part of living communities. We encourage visitors to check the organizer’s latest guidance and be considerate of residents, sellers and local rules.

Our principles

Useful information.
Clear sources.

Trust matters when planning a trip. We prioritize information published by event organizers and local governments, link readers to the original source, and make it clear that event details may change.

How we work

  • Use official organizer pages, local-government pages and public open data.
  • Link to sources and show when information was last checked.
  • Do not reproduce source-page text or photos as listing content.
  • Encourage visitors to confirm details with the organizer before travelling.
